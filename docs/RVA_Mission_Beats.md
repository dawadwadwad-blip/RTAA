# RVA: Aggressive Altruism mission beats

The [campaign plan](RVA_Campaign.md) defines the v2 story and roster. Missions are numbered by story order. Side favors can change help, shortcuts and epilogue details. Main missions carry the evidence required to finish. Decide methods during play where the route, setup or job target can show the trade-off before the player commits.

## Act I · Work Orders

### 01. Night Shift

- Dell gives Mara the keys to a maintenance truck and an overnight striping order for Church Hill. A late dispatch correction has her report to one block below the usual work limit. The printed copy and her truck's instructions share the same order number.
- Take the truck uphill, set the work zone and mark its two assigned segments. The vest keeps routine scrutiny low inside the zone. Crossing the cones with the order active shows a clear boundary warning. A scaffold fire starts offscreen and the worksite signal changes to a request to clear the block.
- Drive the crew out along a marked detour. Someone photographs the city truck while it leaves. Back at the yard, Mara checks in the order and pockets a copy of its dispatch stub. The debrief shows the Night Shift Log entry beside the photo.

### 02. Cold Patch

- The photograph circulates while Dell sends Mara to fill two potholes near Shockoe Bottom. The first job reads as ordinary maintenance. A delivery driver offers to vouch for the truck's arrival time if Mara finishes the blocked loading approach.
- Patch the approach and reopen it to the delivery truck. The work terminal offers a routine diagnostic export. Mara saves a read-only copy and a timestamped receipt before the overnight data rolls off the truck's display.
- A news segment misstates the departure time. The driver can corroborate one part of Mara's timeline. The export becomes available to the player in the crew's tool chest.

### 03. Notice of Public Interest

- Mara drives home to Church Hill and finds the lien notice on the rowhouse. Cal is trying to make payroll and offers to cover a small amount. The filing sketch treats their plot as vacant.
- Walk the block, compare the notice with the corner stakes and bring both to Pike's permit window. The clerk accepts Mara's correction to the occupancy record. Pike can buy a review window and tells her who sponsored the preferred alignment.
- Mara and Cal have time to act. The posted lien amount determines the first consulting offer and the sketch's date becomes part of the story evidence.

### 04. Survey Stakes

- An Albanico Review Board member hires Mara to mark occupied stoops before a survey crew arrives. The Mortar Circle offers more if she also removes the contractor's flags. Both routes use the same steep block and approved boundary.
- A Kind route adds a clearly marked occupied-house overlay and leaves access to the block intact. The Hard route swaps the modeled survey markers on a timer. It delays the contractor and forces a local delivery to divert.
- The Review Board provides a contact or the Mortar Circle provides a favor, according to the job done. The survey poles or the occupied-house boards linger when Mara returns. Use this as the slice's Kind-versus-Hard job.

### 05. Density Bonus

- A Pod crew in Scott's Addition offers paid overtime on a demonstration block. Its model shows affordable units inside a footprint that hides the existing rear-stair access. A resident has the keys to the service yard.
- Move the display pallets and use the forklift to position the sample module. A Kind placement keeps the rear route clear. A Hard placement wins the timed inspection by taking the resident route out of use for the day.
- The Pod pays Mara and offers a wing contact. A site trailer carries a sponsor stamp familiar from the first work order. The player sees who benefited from the convenient placement.

### 06. Overtime

- Dell is ordered to produce Mara's original copy. At closing time the maintenance yard displays a new roster and Mara's hours are missing. A local shop network offers a paid job if she gets a stranded service truck back before the yard shuts.
- Tow the truck through the narrow gate and get a signed return receipt. Use the normal work-order boundary to pass the shift supervisor. At the terminal, compare the job number with a duplicate issued inside the office after Dell signed the roster.
- The forged order originated inside municipal dispatch. The receipt clears today's work but leaves the night shift unresolved. Mara accepts her first regular off-the-books overtime contact.

## Act II · Clients

### 07. Bike Lane

- Dispatch asks Mara to test a route through the VCU and Broad corridor. The Spoke Cell wants an entire turn closed during rush hour. The route map shows which deliveries depend on it.
- A Kind stripe gives traffic a turn pocket and sends riders through a safe marked segment. A Hard closure clears the rider route immediately and diverts the next group of drivers. Watch the response at the next junction before opening the final segment.
- Riders complete the run. Dispatch or the Spoke Cell becomes an active contact. A blocked shop asks Mara to come back later, making the trade-off visible beyond the payout.

### 08. Parking Minimum

- A Southside crossing opens for a timed freight convoy. Asphalt Samaritans ask Mara to recover a stranded pickup. The Hitch will pay her to clear the approach for a private convoy at the same time.
- Tow the pickup into a shoulder bay or use the game's marked closure at the intersection to give the convoy a clear lane. The second route leaves waiting drivers outside a clinic lot.
- The first trip opens the Southside route for this act. Truck-Nutz contacts respond to the choice and Pike's office tests the tow response if Mara earned a citation.

### 09. Closing Time

- Two Carytown shops need the last afternoon delivery. One has a paid loading slot and the other holds an old receipt Mara needs. The Landlord Club offers to reserve the shared bay exclusively.
- Run the delivery loop. A Kind split uses a short carry from the side street to serve both customers. A Hard reservation is a faster single-slot unload that sends the second shop's truck away.
- A dated receipt shows Tate's foundation covering the lease deposit for one shop. The skipped customer leaves a note at the door and alters the next local favor.

### 10. Paper Trail

- The lien review file is about to be archived downtown. A clerk can release a narrow copy, while an outside intermediary wants Mara to bring out the entire review packet.
- Follow the work-room route in daylight and collect the authorized printout or take the after-hours service route for the broader packet. Use site permissions and scheduled access as the play rules. Unrelated residents' pages create a marked Collateral cost on the broader route.
- Both routes yield the same required order-revision record and the sponsor's sign-off. The larger packet provides extra leverage at a price. Neither packet establishes the identity of the person at the terminal.

### 11. First Skeleton

- The delivery receipt lists an address in The Fan that has a bare plastic skeleton and, tonight, a folding chair. If Mara returned the hand truck earlier, she knows where to knock. Otherwise the shopkeeper points out the chair.
- Walk the block and bring a marked bundle of retired case files down to the meeting. A resident offers a courtyard shortcut if Mara already helped with the cart.
- A retired clerk recognizes the Connector's duplicate case number. Mara learns that the council's Standing Committee never formally adjourned and hears the title of the plat she will seek later. The clerk offers a mundane favor before trusting her with records.

### 12. Double Booked

- On one Fan block, Albanico wants old survey markers cleared and the Pod wants new markers installed before morning. Each contact believes Mara is working solely for them.
- The Kind route preserves both boundaries and stages a shared review board at the correct intersection. The Hard route times sign swaps while both crews make rounds. Whichever route is chosen, Mara has to read the block and finish within the same watch.
- The leads show up and thank Mara for different reasons. Both recognize a sponsor's stamp that Tate presented to them as private support. The chosen setup affects which contact will answer a later call.

### 13. Hill Climb

- A cast-iron cornice from a Scott's Addition demolition is due in Church Hill before dawn. The restoration crew paid for a longer, gentler route. A Means contact offers a bonus for meeting the flatbed on the direct grade.
- Secure the cargo and drive uphill. Use the gentler route or accept the faster route and manage a steep switchback and an inspection. A scripted strap warning on the last climb sends Mara to a safe pull-off to re-secure the load.
- Deliver the cornice. Its eventual display follows which crew received it. The shorter vertical slice uses the same loading and hill controls from Shockoe Bottom across one authored climb.

### 14. The Listening Tour

- Pike asks Mara to prep a moving inspection route. The bus will visit a delivery bay, a bus stop and a safer crossing. One crew and the travel time can cover only two stops unless an earned contact volunteers.
- Scout the bus schedule, finish the selected fixes and watch the convoy pass. A skipped stop is still visibly unfinished. Tate arrives as a consulting sponsor and names the faction leaders he has already met.
- Tate pays the original lien through a consulting advance and Mara accepts. The release is dated and shown to Cal. The Connector keeps its preferred alignment and Tate now has a reason to keep calling Mara.

## Act III · Escalation

### 15. Ghost Project

- Mara escorts a load of site materials to a Manchester trailer. The funded alignment onscreen changes as she reaches each gate, while the same cleared parcel and contract numbers keep appearing.
- Carry the stamped work orders to the display board and match the repeated contract identifiers. Then take a simple supply delivery to a property Tate's fund actually rehabilitated and hear from people who use it.
- Reveal the ghost project and the lien trail. Tate's ledger shows 412 helped and 38 harmed in his own accounting. Mara's Net Good ledger appears and backfills previously recorded job outcomes.

### 16. Dead Weight

- Dell starts clearing the tool cage before a budget inspection. The crew needs the remaining gear for service calls, and a crate bears a parcel number Mara knows.
- Help load the equipment into the truck, then take the crate marked for the pension office to a pull-off. At the stop, match the pension receipt, the parcel request and the source stamps.
- Dell admits flagging the rowhouse while moving contract money toward pensions. She confirms who could issue the revision and when. The record distinguishes her role from who started the scaffold fire. Mara can hold the confession privately or share it with Cal.

### 17. Bridge Count

- A Truck-Nutz convoy and a Militia ride meet at opposite approaches to an authored river crossing. A disabled service vehicle leaves one usable route. Both groups need the span open to get home.
- Set segment states at the approaches. A Kind route alternates turns and clears the stranded vehicle. A Hard closure sends one group to the thin alternate route and lets the other finish immediately. Loading and emergency access remain a visible priority in the briefing.
- A closure reroutes existing vehicles after the next decision point. The side that had to wait either answers a later favor or refuses it. Record the result on the bridge's event board.

### 18. Annexation Day

- Mara takes a disputed load to an address near the city–county line. Two fictional authorities claim the stop and issue incompatible directions. The higher city tier makes a straight return to the yard costly.
- Follow the marked county route and see the current tier fall by one at the handoff. Read the new responder's behavior and finish the delivery before crossing back on an eligible route.
- The mechanic sticks for later jobs. The city and county each claim credit for the public work. The disputed boundary remains a pressure rule for any patron.

### 19. Public Comment

- Pike gives Mara a short hearing slot to show a viable route correction. Tenants, shopkeepers and bus riders each bring a different constraint. Committee members can offer a spectacle and extra hands if Mara knows them.
- Haul the model and exhibits to the hearing. An optional supporter can bring the giant skeleton as a witness prop. On the shared floor map, place the three groups' exhibits and protect enough of their time for testimony before the clerk closes the record.
- The clerk accepts a route alternative into the record. The podium stunt can secure an early vote while witnesses secure stronger later backing. The procedural freeze still needs the original plat and a recognized session.

### 20. Freight Delay

- Two patrons want the same cargo moved across the Shockoe rail edge during a timed, authored freight sequence. The route board shows an open crane transfer and a faster detour that will briefly constrain another loading yard.
- A Kind route moves the load with the crane and waits for the safe crossing phase. A Hard route claims the detour first and leaves a competing local delivery queued. Train motion and crossing states are scripted for the set piece.
- The winner gets the handling contract. A delayed operator shows up at the hearing or refuses a later favor. The result stays within the shared hauling and crossing systems.

### 21. Skeleton Crew

- The clerk takes Mara to the Committee basement in The Fan. The assembled households know the original alignment and disagree over using the Files. The meeting needs a quorum and a correct objection.
- Bring the surviving members into the room, put the case sheets in docket order and cite the ordinance that lets the clerk recognize the original plat. Early favors save time. A required hearing notice gives the route and clue to any player.
- Mara leaves with the original plat and a case citation. The plat identifies the holdout parcel and an older public easement. The Committee's powers have a narrow practical use.

### 22. Closing Costs

- Cal has an afternoon deadline to make payroll. He asks Mara to help move his crew's gear out of a yard. During the work a closing courier appears with title papers covering Cal's share of the house.
- Load the last cart with Cal and decide whether to hear his explanation before or after delivery. Mara can repair the relationship or walk off after the job. The workers get a final run to their new site either way.
- Cal sells his share. Tate's company becomes a co-owner and posts a forced-sale notice in the fiction. The posted date and the earlier lien release both remain in the record.

### 23. Rock Bottom

- Mara is suspended and told to turn in her truck. A visible warning names the endangered building if she committed to the Mortar Circle's high-risk protection pact. Its earlier brief spelled out the cost and offered a chance to get the occupants out of the threatened block.
- Return the truck, then fetch the family tool chest from a designated pickup. In the warning branch the fire occurs offscreen and all occupants are accounted for in the regroup scene. Otherwise the sale notice and sealed access take the home out of Mara's hands.
- Mara loses the job and control of the house. Record the site condition and whether Cal or Dell showed up. The Act IV public-use outcome must respect that condition.

## Act IV · Richmond's Future

### 24. Coalition of the Unwilling

- Pike brings Mara the records that were about to be filed away and points out that Tate needs a clean groundbreaking. Three former clients control a crew, an exhibit and access to the first route. Mara needs two supports to start.
- The Kind plan calls in help from people who can vouch for her methods. The Hard plan spends a Files favor to win support under time pressure. If optional allies are scarce, a basic assigned delivery can earn one required crew.
- Pick up the borrowed truck and put the two supports on the rehearsal board. Active contacts and the pressure used carry into the podium eligibility readout.

### 25. Motion to Adjourn

- The retired clerk will revive the old session if Mara brings the original plat and correct docket slip to the municipal floor map. Other attendees push to move the hearing before the last witness arrives.
- Take the materials from the records office to the lectern. Keep an authorized witness on the floor until the clerk calls the item. The Files can shorten one step, while the regular clerk sequence remains available.
- Secure the procedural freeze and cite the older public easement. The Frozen choice becomes available. The team now has a route to a smaller agreement if support and Collateral allow it.

### 26. Gridlock

- A rehearsal convoy jams both routes into the ceremony. The team must get the board, the witness van and Mara's truck to three separate arrival bays.
- The Kind plan opens gaps in sequence and shares one arrival with local deliveries. The Hard plan closes a marked through route to win time at the cost of the public queue. State updates go through the same lane markers used in Bike Lane.
- Save the cleared route and the consequences to the city state. Live queues, a refused favor or a witness arriving early change the next sequence.

### 27. Dell's Last Shift

- Dell is ready to leave the yard. Mara needs the final corroborating statement and the maintenance truck's original record before it changes hands. Dell also promised one last repair to a former crew member.
- Use the borrowed truck to tow the crew member home and deliver the receipts. At the gate, Dell gives Mara a physical return copy and testifies to the dispatch revision. She and Mara confirm which facts the log alone never established.
- The evidence package is complete for every route. Dell agrees to attend, to leave a statement or to go home depending on how Mara treated the crew.

### 28. River Days

- On the waterfront, the speakers rehearse the ribbon-cutting. One resident from Tate's program and one household displaced by it wait at different stops. Mara can bring both to the public plaza.
- Drive the opening-day route and stop for both riders. At the staging bay, the board shows the benefits and costs of the available arrangements. Let the player visit one changed block on the way without spending a mission timer.
- Queue the witnesses and show ending eligibility before the finale. Tate asks Mara to wait until after the cameras turn off to raise the evidence.

### 29. Groundbreaking

- A short convoy chase delivers the evidence truck to the riverfront staging approach. Mara's assigned route and Hi-Vis work order let her through the final pedestrian gate. Tate offers a private deal before she steps onto the podium.
- Put the Night Shift Log beside the dispatch record, Dell's corroboration, the plat and the ledger. Choose one available disposition in front of the audience. The locked options explain exactly which contacts or Collateral condition they lack.
- Resolve the project terms, the fund and control of the parcel. Save the choice before leaving the podium so replaying the next scene preserves it.

### 30. Open House

- Mara takes her mother's tool chest to the surviving porch, a temporary workshop or the agreed public-use site, according to mission 23 and the finale. Cal or Dell is there if the relationship supports it.
- Unload the tools, hear one short exchange and walk to the nearest changed block. See who uses the crossing, what the lot became and which promised public work got built.
- Return control in free roam. Relocations, closed sites, supporter props and brief epilogue cards reflect the ending and Collateral. The plot's closure comes from this one playable visit.

## Ending rules

These are production defaults for tuning. Any numerical ranges belong in mission data once the first slice has playtest evidence.

| Outcome | Availability at the podium | Visible result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Built** | Available through the story | Tate builds his preferred version and keeps the fund operating. Mara takes the offered cut. The route and selected storefronts show what the arrangement displaced. |
| **Frozen** | Earned through required mission 25 | The Committee stops the project and Tate's linked fund freezes. The house site keeps its prior physical state while the stalled alignment and closed offices show the cost. |
| **The Third Alignment** | Low Collateral and active Believer support from at least two groups | Use the easement for a smaller public use and preserve a smaller, independently reported fund. The surviving house can be repaired with consent. A burned site uses the parcel itself and a separate place for the family. |
| **The Fixer** | High Collateral and active Means support from at least two groups | Mara takes Tate's seat. The signboard, contracts and short epilogue show who she funds and which costs she accepts. |

Authored Method Dial alternatives are budgeted for missions 04, 05, 07, 08, 09, 10, 12, 13, 17, 20, 24 and 26. The hearing, audit and finale have their own choices. The other main missions use the same tools and a shorter authored route. Test Strain swaps and routing before committing to additional versions.

## Night Shift first build

The [Church Hill slice checks](RVA_Church_Hill_Slice.md) cover the native Night Shift test, short Hill Climb and Survey Stakes grayboxes.

The [Night Shift graybox](RVA_Night_Shift_Graybox.md) implements the work order, striping and checkpoint flow. Build one walkable Church Hill block around it with an uphill vehicle approach, two striping segments, a fallback exit and a yard return marker. Use the approved pilot heights and existing vehicle setup when available. A graybox scene can test the flow before the streamed city is ready. Reuse the Standing Committee actor on the block as an optional free-roam stop.

| Beat | Player input and world feedback | Complete when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Briefing | Read or acknowledge the work order. Highlight the truck and the first work-zone boundary. | Mara has the order and boards the truck. |
| Striping | Drive into the marked zone. Activate the Line-Striper along segment A and segment B. Distinguish cosmetic paint from a completed segment in the UI. | Both authored segments acknowledge completion once. |
| Worksite closes | The distant fire beat plays through lighting, radio and a site closure. The first exit sign changes and the detour is marked. | Mara clears the site by the detour or its recovery route. |
| Debrief | Park inside the yard zone. Inspect the dispatch stub and the first Night Shift Log entry. | The objective completes once and the two records are available for Cold Patch. |

Before building the next slice jobs, time one clean run and one retry. Verify that crossing the edge with the order equipped changes the work-zone indicator, duplicate stripe events leave completion unchanged, the scripted closure fires once, and reloading the checkpoint recovers the objective and log entries without an extra reward. Keep the alternate exit readable from the truck cab. Aim for 5–8 minutes for this graybox and 20–30 minutes across the three slice jobs and optional skeleton payoff.
